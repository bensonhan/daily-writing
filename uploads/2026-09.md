	I also finished my 11th book of the year, The Netanyahu’s. The book contrasts assimilation (represented by Ruben) and defiance (represented by Ben Zion). Ruben has become a professor at a small community college and has tried to distance himself from his Jewish identity, noting how he doesn’t like how he was chosen to evaluate Ben Zion just because he’s a Jew.
